01 / THE PROBLEMPUBLIC LEDGERS, PRIVATE LIVES

Your salary shouldn't become public data just because payroll moves on-chain.

Verification should answer a question. It should not open a person's entire compensation history.

PayDrip separates what must be proven from what should remain private.

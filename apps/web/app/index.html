WHAT VERIFICATION MEANS

A narrower answer.

A recorded receipt means the PayDrip circuit accepted an authorized, unrevoked record at the time of submission. The issuer can still issue inaccurate off-chain payroll data.

Claim context is a public identifier. The contract does not bind it to a named verifier or a signed request policy.
